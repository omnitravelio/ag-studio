SCOPE

Concept build
Client
Creator teaching freelancers
Problem
Advice that stops at "raise your rates"
Product
Pricing system at $149
Scope
Product plus a custom tool

Freelancers think they have a pricing problem. They have a scoping problem, and the pricing problem is downstream of it. So the product had to do two things: produce a defensible number, then hold it when a client pushes.

The sales page runs a live calculator that tells a visitor exactly how much money they are losing at their current rate. The product itself is not a document at all, it's a searchable console you keep open in a tab, because nobody reads a pricing guide front to back. They open it mid-conversation with a client waiting.
